Are You Making These Sales Mistakes?

Most lost deals don’t come from a bad product. They come from small mistakes in the conversation.

PROBLEM 01

You Talk More Than You Listen.

Customers lose interest when salespeople focus too much on explaining instead of understanding their needs.

PROBLEM 02

You Struggle to Understand Customer Needs.

You don’t always know which questions to ask to discover the real problem.

PROBLEM 03

Customers Say “I’ll Think About It.”

You struggle to move conversations towards a clear buying decision.

PROBLEM 04

Price Becomes Your Biggest Obstacle.

Customers focus on price because they don’t fully understand the value.

PROBLEM 05

You Lose Deals Despite Having a Good Product.

A good product alone does not guarantee a successful sales conversation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly will I receive?+
Access to an interactive learning platform with 8 modules and 21 short lessons: Customer Psychology, SPIN Selling, AIDA, FAB, Challenger, LAER, Sandler and Sales Closing. It includes visual diagrams, real sales conversations, quizzes, objection simulations, and a toolkit with checklists, a SPIN question bank, objection scripts, message templates and WhatsApp scripts.
Is this suitable for beginners?+
Yes. Every framework is explained in simple language with examples first, then practice. If you’re experienced, the SPIN, Challenger and Sandler lessons will sharpen how you run discovery and qualification.
How long will it take to learn?+
The core lessons take about 60 minutes. Your actual time depends on your pace and how much you practise. Many people take one module a day and use the toolkit in real conversations between lessons.
What is SPIN Selling?+
SPIN stands for Situation, Problem, Implication and Need-payoff questions. It was developed by Neil Rackham, whose team at Huthwaite studied more than 35,000 sales calls to find what successful salespeople do differently. It’s the main module in this playbook.
Are practical examples included?+
Yes. Lessons use realistic examples from digital agencies, website development, real estate, SaaS, retail, professional services and B2B sales, with full sample conversations and practice exercises.
Can I use these techniques in WhatsApp sales?+
Yes. Lessons show how to apply each framework in meetings, on calls and on WhatsApp, and the toolkit includes ready-to-adapt WhatsApp templates for first replies, follow-ups, price objections and confirming next steps.
Is this suitable for business owners?+
Absolutely. If you speak to customers, pitch your services or handle enquiries yourself, the playbook helps you ask better questions, explain your value and agree clear next steps.
How do I access the product after payment?+
You pay securely on SuperProfile. After payment, you receive your access link to the learning platform. Open it on your phone or laptop and start learning straight away.
Can I revisit the lessons?+
Yes. You can revisit any lesson and the toolkit whenever you need them. Your progress is saved in the browser on the device you use to learn.
